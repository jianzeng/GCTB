================================================================================
Analysis
================================================================================

Compare the 'Computational time' above for each thread count.

Expected results for this small dataset:
  - 1 thread:  ~6-7 seconds (baseline)
  - 2 threads: ~4-5 seconds (1.3-1.5x speedup)
  - 4 threads: ~3-4 seconds (1.5-2x speedup)
  - 8 threads: ~3-4 seconds (minimal additional benefit)

Why limited speedup?
  - Dataset is small (only 6717 SNPs)
  - Single chromosome (limited parallelization opportunities)
  - Threading overhead significant for small problems

For REAL large-scale data (>50K SNPs, multiple chromosomes):
  - Expected: 4-8x speedup with 4-8 threads
  - Near-linear scaling with chromosome count
